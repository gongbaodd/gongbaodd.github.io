# Estonia Maritime Museum and KGB Cell

I visited the [Estonia Maritime Museum](https://www.meremuuseum.ee/en/) in Tallinn, which is located in the Seaplane Harbour. The museum features a variety of maritime exhibits, including historical ships and submarines. I like the roof top view of the harbour, which is a great place to take photos.

[The KGB Cell](https://vabamu.ee/en/exhibitions/kgb-en/) is around this area, which is a museum dedicated to the history of the KGB in Estonia. The cell is a small, dark room that was used for interrogations and imprisonment during the Soviet era.
