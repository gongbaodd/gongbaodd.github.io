# Fat Magret Museum & KGB Cell 🏰⚓🕵️‍♂️

I visited the [Fat Magret Museum](https://www.meremuuseum.ee/en/) in Tallinn, located in the charming Old Town. 🏙️ The museum has a variety of maritime exhibits, including historic ships and even a submarine! 🚢 My favorite part? The rooftop view of the harbor—it’s a perfect spot for snapping photos. 📸✨

Just around the corner is [The KGB Cell](https://vabamu.ee/en/exhibitions/kgb-en/), a museum dedicated to the history of the KGB in Estonia. 🕶️ The cell is a small, dark room used for interrogations and imprisonment during the Soviet era—a chilling but fascinating experience. ⚡
