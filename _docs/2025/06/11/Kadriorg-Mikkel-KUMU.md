# Kadriorg, Mikkel, and KUMU: Your Perfect Art-Hopping Day in Tallinn! 🎨

Ready for a culture-packed adventure? 🌟 Kadriorg is hands-down one of Tallinn's most gorgeous districts, and trust me – you're going to fall in love with its dreamy parks, fairy-tale palaces, and world-class museums!

## Start at Kadriorg Palace 🏰
First stop: the absolutely stunning Kadriorg Palace! This baroque beauty has been transformed into an art museum, and wow – the oil painting collection is seriously impressive. You could easily spend hours getting lost in all that artistic goodness. The palace itself is Instagram gold, so don't forget your camera! 📸

## Pop Over to Mikkel Museum 🖼️
Just a quick 30-meter stroll from the palace, you'll find the charming Mikkel Museum. It's way smaller and cozier than its grand neighbor, but that's exactly what makes it special! This little gem focuses on Estonian art and culture, with an amazing collection of paintings, sculptures, and decorative arts spanning the 19th and 20th centuries. Perfect for art lovers who want something more intimate! ✨

## End at KUMU for the Modern Vibes 🏢
Now here's where things get really exciting – take a peaceful walk deep into Kadriorg Park (seriously, the park itself is a masterpiece 🌳), and you'll stumble upon this sleek, modern building that's KUMU (Kunstimuuseum). 

This is THE Estonian Museum of Modern Art, and it's absolutely massive! We're talking one of the largest and most important art museums in all of Estonia. The contemporary art scene here is mind-blowing – they showcase incredible work from both Estonian artists and international superstars. Whether you're into cutting-edge installations or thought-provoking exhibitions, KUMU delivers big time! 🎭

**Pro tip:** Make this a full-day adventure – each spot has its own unique vibe, and the park walk between them is pure magic! 🚶‍♀️💫
