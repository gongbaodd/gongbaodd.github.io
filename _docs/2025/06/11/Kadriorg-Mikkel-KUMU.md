# Kadriorg, Mikkel, and KUMU

Kadriorg is a beautiful district in Tallinn, Estonia, known for its stunning parks, palaces, and cultural institutions. Kadriorg Palace is now an art museum, many oil paintings there.

About 30 meters from the palace is Mikkel Museum, which is a smaller museum that focuses on Estonian art and culture. It has a collection of paintings, sculptures, and decorative arts from the 19th and 20th centuries.

Walk deep into the Kadriorg Park, there is a mordern building, which is KUMU (Kunstimuuseum), the Estonian Museum of Modern Art. KUMU is one of the largest and most important art museums in Estonia, showcasing contemporary art and exhibitions from both Estonian and international artists.
