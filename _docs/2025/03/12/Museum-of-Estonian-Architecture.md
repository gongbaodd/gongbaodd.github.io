# Museum of Estonian Architecture

Last weekend, I went to the museum of Estonian Architecture. It is a small museum in between of the old town and the Linnahall. There are four shows. The ground floor, shows different architectures in Estonia after 1900. Second floor shows the architect traveler teams during soviet time. The third floor, shows photographs in the exact position in Tallinn, in 1998, 2008 and 2018(most places were tear down). Under ground, there is a show for children to learn building. You can see, touch and smell different building materials.
