### ✨ My Top Picks & Key Takeaways

I walked away with two standout resources I'm keen to explore further:

* **[GraphFrames](https://graphframes.io/):**
    * This is an impressive **graph API for Apache Spark**.
    * It offers a scalable and efficient way to work with graph algorithms.
    * It presents a compelling alternative to dedicated graph databases like Neo4j, especially when integrating with existing Spark pipelines.

* **[marimo notebook](https://marimo.io/):**
    * A promising, **lightweight alternative to traditional Jupyter Notebooks**.
    * I'm looking forward to diving in and seeing how it improves the interactive data exploration and sharing workflow.

Last time I check some big data stuffs, were Handoop and Hives, it was a great experience connecting with the local data science community and getting a glimpse into the latest developments in these fields.
