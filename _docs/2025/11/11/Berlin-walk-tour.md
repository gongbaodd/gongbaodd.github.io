# 🌃 Berlin by Night: An Insightful Walk Tour

We arrived at the **Berlin Hauptbahnhof (Main Station)** at night. What an impressive structure! 🤯 It's incredibly vast, with trains stopping on different levels, truly giving an "Inception-like" feeling.

![DB](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762972361/IMG_8712_nzykgx.jpg)

---

### **A Cold Welcome? The Cash Conundrum** 🪙

Surprisingly, there was no prominent "Berlin welcomes you" sign for visitors' photos outside. I sometimes find Berlin a bit **"cold"** in its practicality. We had to use *cash* to buy train tickets—one route was €9.10! Having to count out so many coins because there was no POS device for card payment, or even a simple QR code for scanning, was a hassle.

![DB](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762972361/IMG_8713_otaaww.jpg)

---

### **Futuristic Visions and Somber History** 🚶

The area around the station is under construction (a common sight in a growing city!). We walked past **Futurium** ([https://futurium.de/en](https://futurium.de/en)), a fascinating museum dedicated to futuristic environment and living designs.

![Futurium](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762972360/IMG_8716_xgu6o3.jpg)

Crossing a river, we came upon a powerful, yet somber, spot: a memorial dedicated to the **minorities who died during WWII**. A stark reminder of history etched into the city's landscape.

![Memorials for minorities in WWII](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762972359/IMG_8721_cjqoft.jpg)

---

### **The Heart of German Politics** 🇩🇪

Next, we passed the **German Parliament (Reichstag Building)**. I learned there are over 600 seats in the German Parliament—it's a massive legislative body!

![Parliament](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762972359/IMG_8717_yqguhi.jpg)

### **From Wall to Gate: A Divided Past** 🧱

After touring the "western Berlin" view, we arrived at the iconic **Brandenburg Gate**. Look down, and you can see a line of **shining bricks** on the ground—these mark where the infamous **Berlin Wall** once stood.

![The Berlin Wall](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762972359/IMG_8723_wdqrya.jpg)

The famous **Unter den Linden** road is a wide, straight artery designed to allow tanks to travel across Berlin. The massive **Victory Column** states its dominance in what was formerly West Berlin.

![The road](https://res-cloudinary-com.translate.goog/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762972357/IMG_8727_mkwzre.jpg)

![Brandenburg gate](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762972355/IMG_8730_wzmtmj.jpg)

---

### **The Dual City: Legacies of the Split** 🏙️

It was fascinating to see **Humboldt University of Berlin**. I realized that, due to the decades of division, Berlin still maintains a **dual-system style** in many aspects. There are two major Berlin Theatres, and even two of some schools! This organizational redundancy is a direct, enduring legacy of the Wall, thankfully supported by the huge economic scale of the reunited city.

### **East Side Vibrancy and Culinary Rewards** 🍻

We finished the walk near **Alexanderplatz**, which has a different, more vibrant energy.

* **Graffiti Culture:** ![Graffiti](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762972353/IMG_8743_ttoxjb.jpg)
* **Transit Layers:** Seeing the train on the bridge with a tram running underneath it gave me a fleeting, multi-layered "Thailand feeling"! ![train and tram](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762972352/IMG_8742_ibtzea.jpg)

Finally, time for a well-deserved meal! We found a restaurant providing **Munich (München) food and music**.

![Restaurant near Alexandar Platz](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762972353/IMG_8745_cau1gu.jpg)

I *finally* ate the famous **Schweinshaxe** (pork knuckle)! It was €20, and the Radler was €6.80. A bit expensive, perhaps, but absolutely **delicious**! 🤤 The potatoes were soft and wonderfully chewy, almost like small savory breads.

![Schweinshaxe](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762972351/8D580303-5DA6-4394-A3C7-226598BC18BE_kbaldh.jpg)
