# ✈️ Fly to Brandenburg

It’s finally time for my Erasmus trip to Brandenburg! A small journey through four cities — Tallinn, Riga, Berlin, and finally Brandenburg.

---

## 🇪🇪 Tallinn Airport

Waiting at Tallinn Airport is surprisingly fun! 🎹 There’s a **piano** you can play, **two table tennis tables**, and even a **free gym** inside the airport.  

And a fun fact — smoking rooms *do* exist in some European airports! Meaning yes, you can actually bring your lighter on board. 🔥

![piano](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762804441/piano_cmkxgy.jpg)
![gym](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762804422/gym_fybnuv.jpg)

When the plane took off, I finally saw the **sunlight above the clouds** — something rare after weeks of grey skies in Tallinn. ☁️✨  
The clouds looked like giant marshmallows. I always wonder how pilots see anything through them...

![clouds](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762804423/cloud_nonyfs.jpg)

---

## 🇱🇻 Riga Airport

What shocked me most in Riga Airport? A **giant bear** display showing what items are illegal to bring into Europe. 🧸🚫

![bear](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762804877/bear_z5caaz.jpg)

You can buy **mini 50ml whisky bottles** and **cigarettes** right at the duty-free shop — classic European travel essentials. 🥃🚬

![whisky](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762804883/whisky_wswsq4.jpg)
![cigar](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762804878/cigar_kranbs.jpg)

I had a 3-hour layover here. Because of new entry rules for non-EU residents, I decided to stay in the airport. Luckily, Riga Airport has **comfortable seats with charging ports (Type-C and Type-A)** and a great view. 🔌✨

![resting seats](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762804880/resting_seats_lnra49.jpg)

I also spotted some **stylish airplanes** on the runway.

![nice plane](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762804880/stylish_plane_usktaw.jpg)

Even more exciting — **AirBaltic offers free Starlink Wi-Fi! 🚀** The connection is fast enough for video calls, which feels futuristic at 30,000 feet.

![starlink](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762804879/starlink_dpzdrq.jpg)

During the safety video, I even saw **VR headsets** mentioned — not allowed during takeoff, but still cool to see aviation adapting to new tech. 🥽✈️

![headsets](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762804877/headsets_fuzf4l.jpg)

---

## 🇩🇪 Berlin Airport

My first impression of Berlin Airport? Honestly… big but **a bit dirty** 😅.  

I couldn’t even find a “**Welcome to Berlin**” sign — kind of disappointing for such a famous city.  

![The train](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762806115/IMG_8643_yfbovq.jpg)

The **public transport system** here is confusing — multiple companies, multiple apps, and two types of ticketing systems. 🤯 Even worse, some apps don’t fully support English, making it frustrating for tourists.

![information board](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762806116/IMG_8645_agiopg.jpg)

And yes, the ticket machines are literally running on **unactivated Windows systems**. 💀💻

![ticket service](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762806492/IMG_8642_jvtbhq.jpg)

---

## 🏡 Arrived at Brandenburg

After almost **2 hours on the train**, I finally arrived in Brandenburg — tired and starving. It was **11 p.m. on a Sunday**, so most restaurants were closed. Luckily, I found a place called *Palace Pizza* — a **kebab pizza for €10** saved my night. 🍕❤️

![kebab pizza](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762806116/IMG_8661_quzpme.jpg)

Brandenburg isn’t actually that small — its **market size feels bigger than Tallinn**, and surprisingly, **everyday items in the cheapest level are cheaper** too. 💶 I’ll share more about the city in my next post!
