# 1944 (2015)

*1944* is a powerful WWII movie that captures a unique part of Estonian history — a time when the country was caught fighting **on both sides** of the war.  

The story begins with Estonian soldiers fighting under the **German army** retreating from Narva, and later story shifts to those serving in the **Soviet forces** advancing toward Saaremaa.  

Unlike many war films that try to cover too much and lose focus, *1944* keeps its narrative clear and human. Despite featuring **three languages** — Estonian, German, and Russian — the story remains easy to follow and emotionally engaging.  

🎬 A must-watch for anyone interested in WWII history or Estonia’s complex past.
