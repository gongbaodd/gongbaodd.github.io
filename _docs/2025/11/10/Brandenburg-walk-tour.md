# 🚶‍♂️ Brandenburg Walk Tour

Brandenburg is a **quiet, conservative city** about 70 km from Berlin. Compared to Berlin’s fast-paced, international vibe, locals here live **closer to nature** and prefer a slower rhythm of life. However, tourism isn’t a major focus — most people here **don’t speak English**, which makes the city feel more authentically local.

---

## 🏨 Hotel

There aren’t many hotels in the city. The one I stayed in is **just across the street** from the university campus. It offers a **student discount** — €55 per night, including breakfast. 🥐☕

## 🏰 Old Town

Brandenburg has **two historical centers**:  
- The *Old Town*, built around the **15th century**  
- The *New Town*, from the **17th century**

The city wall has long been torn down, leaving only the **gate tower** standing as a reminder of the past.  

![Gate tower](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762850256/IMG_8671_xnszud.jpg)

The Old Town is full of **beautiful brick architecture**, giving the whole area a warm, timeless feel.  

![Cathelic](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762850256/IMG_8676_ttk2vs.jpg)
