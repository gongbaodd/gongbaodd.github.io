# 🎲 Digiwise Gamejam Weekend

This weekend, I had the pleasure of mentoring at [Shokkin’s Digiwise Gamejam](https://et.shokkin.org/digiwise-game-evenings-tallinn/) 💡  

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762187091/Screenshot_2025-11-03_182105_wxdz7l.png)

The jam focused on **tabletop board games**, exploring two meaningful themes: 🧠 *media literacy* and 💬 *cyberbullying awareness*.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762187122/IMG_8572_anczpz.jpg)

After two intense days of creativity and teamwork, each group presented a fully developed board game — and the results were amazing! 🙌  

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1762187123/IMG_8575_bfrna0.jpg)

I was especially happy to see teams experiment beyond traditional card-based RPGs — some went for **map-based** mechanics and even **tile-placement** strategies. 🗺️✨  

A weekend full of learning, creativity, and great people ❤️
