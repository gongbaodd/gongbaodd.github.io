# Week 39: Light Show ✨ @Kadriorg

Every year, Kadriorg Park hosts a special light show 🌌.  

This time, I went straight after class at TLU. At 8 p.m., the tram was sooo packed 🚋🙅, so I just walked — luckily it’s only 1km away.

![Tram](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1758367636/IMG_8149_a4qton.jpg)

Arriving at Kadriorg, the first thing I saw was this glowing entrance 🌟

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1758367623/IMG_8152_kscfas.jpg)

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1758367659/IMG_8155_xhjdr1.jpg)

Walking into the forest felt surreal — so dreamy 🌲💫.  
Though, my phone camera kinda made it look like a horror movie 👀😂

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1758367603/IMG_8158_a268cg.jpg)

I actually missed the first light show around the lake 😅 Too many people squeezed in.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1758367666/IMG_8161_qly8ey.jpg)

Thankfully, the show replays every 40 minutes ⏳. So I just chilled on the grass 🍃, waited… and slowly lost all feeling in my legs 🪑➡️🦵💤

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1758367601/IMG_8165_xr8wt8.jpg)

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1758367632/IMG_8169_kifr3a.jpg)
