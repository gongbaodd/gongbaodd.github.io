# Week 40: TLU Japanese Cafe

Last week I finally joined TLU’s **Japanese Cafe**! 🍵✨  
I had wanted to attend since last year, but back then my schedule clashed with the event. This time, no conflicts — so I went straight in!

---

## 🃏 How it Works  
We were divided into small groups. On each table there were **cards with questions** written in both Japanese and English — great little icebreakers! ⏱️ When the timer rang, we switched groups and topics.  

Some sample topics:  
- *What part-time job would you take?* 💼  
- *What car do you wish to have?* 🚗  

One Japanese student even shared that she works as a **maid in a café** — everyone found it super interesting!

---

## 🍱 Japanese Evening  
Another highlight was the **Japanese Evening**. People shared food, stories, and even taught us some dances. 💃🕺 The most surprising part? Some students wore their **traditional clothes** — really added to the vibe! 👘

---

## 🇨🇳 vs 🇯🇵 Events  
I’ve also attended Chinese events organized by both the Mainland and Taiwan governments. Honestly, those were a bit *too serious* 🤓 compared to the Japanese ones. The Japanese side feels much more relaxed, fun, and effective in **cultural exchange**. Truly, they’re advanced in culture exporting. 🌏✨
