# 🤖 EstoniAI Meetup

Yesterday I joined the [EstoniAI Meetup](https://luma.com/tn3tl3dc?tk=3iFl3O&utm_source=weblnk) — this time the theme was all about **hardware** ⚙️.  
Since I’ve played a bit with [Edge AI](https://www.growgen.xyz/plan/2025/08/02/week-32-edge-impulse), I thought: why not apply? 🚀

What really caught my eye 👀 was [**Lendurai**](https://lendurai.com/), a super cool drone company 🛸.  
They’re doing some interesting work on **drone vision simulation** — which instantly clicked for me since I’m also building a drone simulator.  
Even though the main talk was more about **on-device AI** 🧠, I really hope they’ll show up at **EEVR** in the future.

---

💡 One thing that stood out:  
Here in Europe 🇪🇺, people care *a lot* about **privacy** 🔒. That’s why on-device AI gets so much love.  

From my own experience:  
- A **server** 💻 → usually computes better,  
- A **local device** 📱 → always reacts faster.  

So the real challenge is **balance ⚖️ + transparency 🌐**, which matter way more when privacy is at stake.
