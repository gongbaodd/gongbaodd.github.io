## 🧠 Tech Talk: XR & AI

### The State of XR 🕶️
XR is in a weird spot. The "Metaverse" dream feels far away because the hardware isn't quite there yet.
* **The Future:** Apple defined the "spatial screen." VR headsets will remain niche for gamers, but **VR/AR glasses** will eventually thrive once the price drops.
* **Interaction:** I’m betting on **sEMG bands** (muscle-sensing bands) combined with smart glasses. That’s the final form: lightweight visual + intuitive control.

### The AI Dilemma 🤖
At the start of 2025, everyone thought AI would steal jobs. Reality check: Companies are laying people off not because AI replaced them, but because they ran out of money trying to make AI smarter.
* **The Quality Gap:** In smaller tech markets (like here), AI output might be "good enough." But for top-tier tech, it falls short.
* **The Risk:** AI code is risky. Look at **Next.js**—everyone used the React Server Component feature until severe flaws were found from v12 to v15. If AI writes the code, who is responsible for the flaws? We saw internet services break down multiple times late this year. We need to wake up to this in 2026.
