# A Journey Through Estonian History and Heritage: Maarjamäe Palace, Sagadi Forest, and Palmse Manor 🏰🌲🎬

## 🇪🇪 Estonian History Museum (Maarjamäe Palace)

To wrap up my Erasmus project, I visited the **Estonian History Museum**, spectacularly housed in the **Maarjamäe Palace**. This location offers a breathtaking vantage point for capturing a full panorama of Tallinn city! 📸✨

![city view](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1765115365/IMG_8124_sihqyf.jpg)

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1765115365/IMG_8121_zjkvtt.jpg)

The history museum's exhibitions are structured chronologically, starting right from the **Estonian War of Independence**.

![castle](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1765115365/IMG_8131_fpkjql.jpg)

* **Timeline Highlights:** I saw exhibits showcasing daily life, from traditional city homes to farm life, and the evolution of clothing from the Independence War era to local traditional wear.

| Period/Theme | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| Independence Era | Uniforms and daily life during the War of Independence. |
| Farm Life | Artifacts depicting rural Estonian life. |
| Traditional Clothing | Examples of local attire. |

## 📽️ Estonian Film Museum

Located near the History Museum, the Film Museum underscores cinema's significant role in Estonian culture. It's filled with remarkable film equipment and interactive displays. 🎬

* A look at various film devices and the magic of filmmaking.
* Getting a glimpse behind the scenes with the green screen setup for Computer-Generated Imagery (CGI).

## 🌳 Sagadi Forest Museum

As part of the Erasmus project, a visit to the **Sagadi Forest Museum** was essential, given that forests cover over 50% of Estonia! This museum beautifully illustrates the natural landscape and the traditional ways locals utilized the forest resources.

* The entrance features a charming restaurant, very nice black garlic ice cream. 🍽️
* Exhibits include specialized clothing for various forest-related trades:

| Profession | Attire Displayed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Lumberjack** | Rugged, practical work clothes 🪓 |
| **Beekeeper** | Protective gear for honey collection 🐝 |

![black garlic ice cream](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1765130783/IMG_9014_vtermn.jpg)

## 🖼️ Palmse Manor

**Palmse Manor** stands as one of the most magnificent baroque mansions in all of Estonia, a truly grand piece of architectural history! 👑

* The exterior is stunning, set against the Estonian landscape.
* The interiors, including the main hall, are richly decorated.
* We had a special treat when one of our group members performed a perfect piano concert in the museum—a beautiful moment! 🎹🎶
