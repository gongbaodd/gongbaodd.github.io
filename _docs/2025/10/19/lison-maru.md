# The Sinking of Lisbon Maru

This documentary dives deep into a lesser-known story from WWII — one that happened on the eastern side of the world. 🌏 When Japan conquered Hong Kong, they imprisoned the local British soldiers and sent them to Japan aboard the *Lisbon Maru*.

The ship carried nearly **2,000 prisoners** across the East China Sea when it was tragically **torpedoed by an American submarine**, unaware it was a POW transport. 💥 As the ship sank, the Japanese crew reportedly tried to prevent the prisoners from escaping. But what happened next was nothing short of heroic — **local Chinese fishermen** risked everything, sailing into danger to rescue over **800 British soldiers**, despite being under Japanese occupation. 🐟💪

Those brave fishermen even helped **three survivors disguise themselves in traditional Chinese clothes**, hiding them from the Japanese. 👕👒 Against all odds, these three men managed to flee across half of China — from **Shanghai to Chongqing** — to make sure the world would hear this incredible story. ✨

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1760906403/p2917440895_wvcvap.webp)

Seeing the photo of these three “locals” in disguise is both touching and a little funny — three white guys dressed head to toe in traditional Chinese outfits! 😂 But behind the humor lies an unforgettable story of courage, humanity, and cross-cultural solidarity in the darkest of times. ❤️
