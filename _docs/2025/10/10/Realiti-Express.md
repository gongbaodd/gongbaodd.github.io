# Realiti Express 2025 🚀

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1760187186/IMG_8364_llbpk9.jpg)

Time flies faster than your VR headset battery 🔋💨  
Last year, I joined [the first Estonian EEVR meetup](/plan/2024/10/20/week-42-xr-estonia).  
Now it’s year two — still the same reality, but a little more *express*. 😎

Last year was like a **Titan battle** ⚔️ — Apple Vision Pro vs Meta Quest 3.  
This year? Meta showed up alone with its Ray-Ban Glasses 😎— and they’re still in “demo mode” (you can try them in-store… if you survive the queue 🕰️).

So yes, this year’s theme is **less hardware flex, more software zen**. 💻✨

---

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1760187189/IMG_8373_yuiltt.jpg)

First up, [Concept VR](https://www.konceptvr.com/) — showing how to turn VR into a **panoramic documentary machine**.  

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1760187187/IMG_8365_axwxf7.jpg)

TalTech brought their new **Unitree G1** — friend from home.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1760187187/IMG_8383_ufkv7t.jpg)

And yes, here’s **The Meta Ray-Ban Display** — cool shades, limited pixels. 🕶️✨

---

[Maru VR](https://maruvr.ee/) showed off updates for *Bootstrap Island* (still PCVR only — Quest users, keep dreaming 🏝️).  

[Unloop VR](https://www.uploadvr.com/unloop-hands-on-sci-fi-puzzling-on-repeat/) brought a slick sci-fi escape room powered by Unreal’s MetaSound and Blueprints.  
But honestly... I bet **Godot** could do the same thing.

[Alpha 3D](https://www.alpha3d.io/) seems to be cooking with **Stable DreamFusion** to make 3D assets from thin air (or GPU smoke 💨).

And Helsinki XR Centre shared their [XR roadmap](https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVL7Ffy2M=/)

Also, there are some defence techs. I am not a war person, and a lot defence techs they do not share so much. So there are not so much to learn.
