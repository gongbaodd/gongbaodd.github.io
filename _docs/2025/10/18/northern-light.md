# 🌌 My First Northern Light Experience

Ever since I started thinking about studying abroad, I *strategically* chose Nordic countries — not just for education, but for a very scientific reason: **I wanted to see the Northern Lights!** 💚✨  

But fate had jokes to tell. In my first year, there was a massive aurora visible all across Europe... at **2 A.M.** 🕑  
Guess who slept through it? 😴 Yup, me.

So I installed [My Aurora Forecast & Alerts](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.jrustonapps.myauroraforecast&hl=en-US) — hoping to redeem myself. Most of the alerts? False alarms. Until one night last month… I actually got the real notification.  
And what did I do? I **dismissed it.** 🙃 Another missed aurora. Another “me problem.”

This time though, I was ready. ⚡ As soon as the alert popped up, I grabbed my clothes and *ran*. One of the best viewing spots is the beach near Linnahall — the same place I usually go swimming 🏊‍♂️.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1760905081/IMG_8445_hmfhh1.jpg)

The forest was *way* darker than it looks in the photo — I could barely see the road. 🌲🕯️

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1760905080/IMG_8449_f0iwvu.jpg)

But once I reached the beach… there it was. The faint green light shimmering above the sea. 🌊💚 No streetlights. No noise. Just magic.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1760905076/IMG_8453_lfgdmk.jpg)

I even tried moving to the platform in front of Linnahall — big mistake. More city lights, less aurora, and a nearby restaurant blasting music that could kill any sense of cosmic wonder. 🎶🚫
