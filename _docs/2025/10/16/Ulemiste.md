# 🚗 Road Trip to Ülemiste City  

Today I joined [the Road to Ülemiste City](https://www.ulemistecity.ee/en/service/road-trip-to-ulemiste-city-hub-2/) — a tour through one of the most innovative business hubs in Tallinn! 💼✨  

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1760734233/IMG_8411_xlpvpl.jpg)

We visited several exciting workplaces, including [Playtech](https://www.playtech.com/) 🎮 and [Fujitsu](https://global.fujitsu/et-ee/local/about) 💻.  

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1760734233/IMG_8413_paf0tb.jpg)
![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1760734242/IMG_8415_pf1xwl.jpg)

But the **real highlight** of the day wasn’t digital — it was *delicious*! 🍴  
At lunch, I had the **best meal since coming to Estonia** — *Roujiamo (肉夹馍)*, a steamed pancake stuffed with grilled BBQ meat. 🔥 absolutely perfect. 😋
