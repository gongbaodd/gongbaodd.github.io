# I Am Low IQ for the First Time 🤯

## The Dream Team That Couldn’t
This summer, I got an *“internship offer”* from a group who claimed they would **revolutionize hiring**.  
After a few chats, I realized they might actually not worked once:  
- no experience ✅  
- no skills ✅  
- no money ✅  
- ...but a **big idea to change the world** 🌍  

So I politely refused. (And by “politely,” I mean I ran.)

---

## The Test That Tested My Faith in Humanity
Fast forward a few weeks.  
Another company reached out — the one that asked for my **20-year-old high school transcript** (yes, really).  
They sent me a link to the [Thomas GIA Test](https://www.thomas.co/assessments/general-intelligence-assessment-gia).  

I thought, “Sure, why not?”  
Then I opened it.  
It was close to purple 💜...

Clearly **not AI-generated**, because no AI would dare produce such terrible UX.  
The test looked like something between *kindergarten worksheets* and *gorilla enrichment puzzles*:  

- “Wang is taller than Zhang. Who is shorter?”  
- “Find how many characters are pronounced the same.”  
  ![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1760190183/IMG_8347_frl5jp.jpg)
- “Find the furthest number.”  
  ![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1760190182/IMG_8341_xnrtvg.jpg)

They even asked me to use my **native language** for accuracy.  
Which is cute — except their “translation” was so bad I had to translate it *back into English* to understand what it meant. 🤦‍♂️  

---

## The Humiliation
I was bored.  
I’m a programmer. I can do calculus in my head.  
These questions were so simple they made me question **their** IQ, not mine.  

Then... the twist.  
That afternoon, I got an email:  
> “We regret to inform you... you did not pass the test.”

Wait. WHAT?! 😳  

---

## My “Low IQ” Report
I downloaded my “results.”  
There are five test areas:  
- Perceptual Speed  
- Reasoning  
- Number Speed & Accuracy  
- Spatial Visualization  
- Word Meaning  

I scored *above average* in four — but *below* in one.  
Guess which?  
**“Word Meaning.”**  

So apparently, I failed because I couldn’t understand *their* bad Chinese translations.  
Honestly, that’s fair. Even **Google Translate** would cry over that mess. 😂  

---

## The Rabbit Hole 🕳️
Being a Digital Learning Game student, I got curious.  
How did this “test” even come to exist?  

Turns out it was born in the **late 1980s**, a commercial adaptation of the **British Army Recruitment Battery (BARB)**.  
Yes — it was literally a military IQ test turned corporate HR tool.  

Even more shocking?  
**No scientific paper**, **no peer-reviewed journal**, and **no transparency** in 50 years.  
Even the **MBTI** (the astrology of psychology) has *more* research behind it! 🧠✨  

---

## Cultural Mismatch Madness
The test proudly claims to be “localized.”  
Let’s see:  

- In Chinese, testers count **characters with the same pronunciation**.  
  ![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1760190183/IMG_8347_frl5jp.jpg)
- In English, testers find **capitalized letters**.  
  ![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1760192907/Thomas-GIA-Perceptual-Speed-Sample-2_ubugis.webp)

Totally equal, right? 😅  
(52 English letters vs. *literally millions* of Chinese characters.)  

They did not separate the data with languages. Surely I am average in the other 4 tests among my home people.

---

## HR’s Favorite Toy 🧩
Despite this, many HRs swear by the GIA test.  
They call it “scientific.” I call it **pseudo-science wrapped in corporate purple**.  

If they really believe this measures intelligence, then maybe it’s actually a **loyalty test** —  
to find the people who will **do anything without questioning the boss**. 🙇‍♂️  

---

## Final Thoughts 💭
They bragged about having *“80,000+ applicants every month.”*  
(Why does every company love this number? It’s not even that impressive.)  

Europe once led the world in logic and innovation.  
Now it’s letting **unscientific, outdated IQ tests** decide who gets hired.  

If this is the new standard for intelligence,  
then I’m proud to say —  
**I’m officially low IQ.** 🥳
