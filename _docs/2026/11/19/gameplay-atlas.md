# Gameplay Atlas: Care & Sustain

Recently, I found this website made by [@onefifthblue](https://x.com/onefifthblue).
It disassembles small mechanics from games across different genres.
Sometimes when I decide to make a game,
making one mechanic is not hard.
But one mechanic cannot fit the scale of a modern game.
Thinking through multiple mechanics takes time and energy.
That is where this website comes in.
It is like a dictionary,
so this series will start reading and collecting these core mechanics.

Let's start with "Care and Sustain":

## Topics

Under the theme of "Care and Sustain" are farming, fishing, cooking, mining, survival, and breeding.

They are like resource management: keeping plants and land healthy, kitchen preparation, and maintaining mental health...

### Farming

Core loops:

- Ground and planting
- Add seasons (timeline)

- Irrigation coverage (toolkit upgrade)
- Fertilizer investment (toolkit upgrade)

- Seed reinvestment
- Crop–substrate matching (match the soil with the plant)
- Crop protection
- Livestock care

### Mining

Mining is quite like survival; the two themes share some mechanics.

- Harvesting tool gates (you need the right tool to start)
- Resource detection overlays
- Volumetric excavation
- Oxygen-budgeted gathering
- Gathering quotas and stopping decisions
- Gathering to sustain gathering
- Clues to buried deposits
- Dismantling for salvage (reusing tools as resources)
- Revisiting renewable forage
- Depth-targeted mining (different depths yield different resources)

### Survival & needs

- Competing survival needs
- Collecting and purifying water
- Fatigue and sleep planning
- Bleeding and urgent bandaging
- Wound hygiene and infection prevention
- Inspecting the body to find problems (watch out for insects or viruses)
- Food balance and nutrition
- Stress buildup and crisis
- Wetness, cold, and warming up
- Injured limbs change capabilities
- Temporary symptom relief
- Interrupting disease transmission

### Breeding & ecology

This is like farming, but with hybridization.

- Arranging neighbors to breed plants
- Selecting parents for offspring traits
- Preserving genetic diversity
- Gradually taming wild animals
- Controlling breeding and population size
- Checking habitat capacity before adding animals
- Planning which species can share a home
- Preparing conditions that enable breeding
