# Glehni Park & Chamber Tea

Attended Larsen's hiking club. Today, we went to Glehni Park.

Right now, most of the park is owned by TalTech, including the castle and the tower.

![Castle](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1795377162/IMG_4666_knmwfw.jpg)

Glehn built this park and the sculpture of the Estonian giant hero Kalevipoeg fighting a dragon. Yes, the crocodile in the picture is supposed to be a dragon, but Estonians don't buy it. They call the sculpture Glehn's Devil.

![Glehni Kurat](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1795377161/IMG_4669_dhrqug.jpg)

![The dragon](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1795377162/IMG_4673_m0e8qt.jpg)

![The tower](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1795377161/IMG_4674_yhf0ss.jpg)
