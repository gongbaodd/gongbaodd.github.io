# Gameplay Atlas: Develop & interact

These genres basically focus on storytelling. I used to make [an interactive novel - 1944](/plan/2025/12/14/week-51-pannellum/). I really wish I had read this part before making it.

## Topics

The themes here are progression, relationships, cooperation, and dialogue.

### Progression & legacy

- Exclusive specialization branches (different skill branches)
- Practice-based skill growth
- Refund and reassign upgrades
- Persistent currency between attempts (roguelike)
- Choose the next heir (roguelike)
- Reset progress for lasting acceleration
- Escalating challenge conditions (harder levels)
- Random choices at level-up
- Complete a weapon evolution recipe
- Research prerequisite chains (skill → weapon upgrades)
- Death costs trained abilities
- Progress through learned knowledge

- Communicating split information (one piece of information split among different people)
- Hidden roles and convincing behavior
- Discussion, evidence and voting
- Reviving a downed teammate
- Crewing a shared vehicle
- Moving while physically tethered
- Reaching separate controls and synchronizing
- Combining different character abilities
- Temporary alliances and betrayal

### Dialogue & event choices

- Answer before the response window closes
- Choose silence and let the other person continue
- Use evidence to challenge a specific statement
- Let an earlier choice change a later event
- Allocate limited time among leads (separate work with one timeline)
- Open extra dialogue through identity and experience
- Respond through what you make and serve
- Continue earlier consequences through another character
- Repeat a time window with a different event plan
- Intervene before the other person finishes
