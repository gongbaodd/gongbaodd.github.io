# München: A Travel Guide Straight from the Textbook

Last year, while studying German, one of my lessons focused entirely on a bike trip through Munich. Having that mental map made planning this trip feel like second nature—it was like walking right into my own notes.

## From Baroque Beauty to Dancing Puppets

![Asamkirche](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1770933743/IMG_1473_lvhpit.jpg)

On my way to **Marienplatz**, I stumbled upon **Asamkirche**. This church is a hidden gem; its ornate Baroque facade stands out sharply against the surrounding buildings. Best of all? It’s free to enter.

![Marienplatz](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1770933742/IMG_1478_ti3ihc.jpg)

<iframe src="https://youtube.com/shorts/XHD8zQg8KC4?si=Oukryz3uPGCL9udX" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen></iframe>

I made it to the main square just in time for the 11 AM **Glockenspiel** at the New Town Hall (Rathaus). Watching the copper puppets reenact scenes from Munich's history is a classic "must-do," and it didn't disappoint.

## Local Bites and City Legends

![Vinzenzmurr](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1770933739/IMG_1513_ejeckg.jpg)

For lunch, I stopped at **Vinzenzmurr**, a local favorite for quick eats. I ordered two sandwiches, and the *Fleischpflanzerl Semmel* (a Bavarian-style meatball roll) was incredible.

The area around Marienplatz is dense with history. There are countless churches to explore—all free and worth the detour. I especially loved the local "urban legends": there are fountains where you can wash your wallet to attract wealth and sculptures you can touch to find love. I adore European cities that keep these quirky anecdotes alive.

## Surfers and English Gardens

Passing through the **Hofgarten**, I reached the **Eisbachwelle**, where people actually surf on a standing wave in the middle of the city! My textbook called this area the **English Garden** and mentioned the famous **Chinese Tower**. To be honest, the tower felt a bit smaller in person than I had imagined.

![Chinese Tower](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1770933738/IMG_1546_fqo32j.jpg)

Nearby stands the **Siegestor** (Victory Gate). Half of the gate was destroyed by bombing during WWII, and the locals chose to leave it partially ruined as a monument to peace and a reminder of the war.

![Siegestor](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1770933740/IMG_1551_pqknaj.jpg)

## The Schweinshaxe Dream

Eating *Schweinshaxe* (pork knuckle) in Munich has always been a dream of mine. While restaurants charge upwards of €19.90, I found a "mini" version for just **€4.00** in Lidl. It tasted amazing and was the perfect budget-friendly way to tick that off my bucket list.

![mini haxen](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1770933740/IMG_1559_hfjnsf.jpg)

## Final Thoughts

There is still so much left to see—the BMW Museum and the Mercedes-Benz Museum will have to wait for my next visit. As I boarded my train to Prague, I was treated to the Bavarian countryside. Rolling hills and rustic farms looked just like a landscape painting.
