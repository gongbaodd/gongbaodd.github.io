# Madrid Vibes & Masterpieces at the Prado 🇪🇸🎨

Finally arrived in Madrid! 📍 After a bit of a rainy start that left all my clothes soaked, I had to get creative. My hostel offers laundry for €9, which is a lifesaver, but while everything was in the wash, I had to go out for breakfast wearing the only dry things I had left: a **swimsuit and a winter jacket.** 👙🧥 Quite the fashion statement for the Spanish winter!

![Me](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1770505250/IMG_0826_wfatn8.jpg)

## First Impressions: The City 🏙️

Walking around the city center, Madrid feels surprisingly brand new. Despite being the capital for over 400 years, it’s all wide roads and grand, polished buildings rather than ancient ruins.

![Palacio Real de Madrid](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1770505252/IMG_0872_luf6ap.jpg)

* **Palacio Real de Madrid:** They say it’s one of the largest palaces in Europe, but honestly? It felt a bit compact! The gardens are beautiful though, and completely free to wander. 🌳

![Parque de el Retiro](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1770505256/IMG_0897_yp7yma.jpg)

* **Parque de el Retiro:** This is the heart of the city. There’s a massive square lake, street musicians filling the air with tunes, and tons of locals jogging. It’s the perfect spot to decompress. 🎶🏃‍♂️

---

## The Main Event: Museo del Prado 🖼️✨

The Prado is conveniently located right near the park. Pro tip: tickets are free after 18:00, but the line was absolutely massive, so I opted to pay the **€15** the next day to actually have time to see it.

**Here is the lowdown on the Prado experience:**

![Museo del Prado](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1770505250/IMG_0911_h0zblb.jpg)

* **It’s Huge:** I spent **4 hours** wandering through the galleries and still didn’t see everything. The scale of the collection is mind-blowing! 🤯

* **The Tech Struggle:** Warning! There is almost **zero data signal** inside. I wanted to use AI as my personal tour guide, but the signal just wouldn't cooperate. Luckily, the special exhibition area has free Wi-Fi! 📶

* **No Photos Allowed:** This was a bit frustrating. The Prado is very strict about cameras—even if you're just trying to snap a photo of the name tag to know the artist. 🚫📸 I believe museums should be about spreading knowledge, but they are very old-school here. I think museum should provide multiple options for different people. For example, I am more interested in the materials on the artwork than the story of it. The modern AI can do that.

![Muse Muse](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1770505254/IMG_0944_ezthd0.jpg)

* **The Best Hack:** Since my AI plan failed, I downloaded **MuseMuse**. It’s a free walking tour app and the experience was a solid **5 stars!** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Highly recommend it for navigating the maze of masterpieces.

## A Note on Spanish Food & Travel 🥘🚆

![food](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1770505251/IMG_0844_tlw2bx.jpg)

* **The Diet:** Spanish food is tasty, but I’m desperately craving some green! I wish they’d throw a few more onions or tomatoes into the mix. 🥗

![station](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1770505258/IMG_0961_dtjktq.jpg)

* **Transport Lessons:** I headed to **Atocha station** only to find my train was cancelled due to recent railway maintenance. I booked through Trip.com, and while they emailed me, the messages were all in Spanish and I thought they were just ads! 📩❌

* **Pro Tip:** Always book through the **official railway website**. Third-party apps like Omio or Trip.com are a nightmare when it comes to getting refunds for cancellations. Luckily, I stayed calm and booked a bus instead. This isn't my first rodeo with cancelled trains on this trip! 🚌💨
