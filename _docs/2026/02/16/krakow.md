### Exploring the City 🚋

Krakow feels more modernized than many other European cities I've visited. Their transport hubs are true "market centers"—the main train station is literally inside a massive shopping mall! 🛍️ I'm staying right in the sweet spot between the station and the Old Town.

![Bazylika Mariacka](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1771183316/IMG_1957_r7dsn5.jpg)

At the top of **St. Mary's Basilica (Bazylika Mariacka)**, a trumpeter plays every hour 🎺. You’ll notice the music stops abruptly in the middle. This honors a 13th-century trumpeter who was shot in the throat by an arrow while sounding the alarm during a Mongolian invasion. A haunting but beautiful tradition. 🏹

## Deep Dive: The Wieliczka Salt Mine 🧂⚒️

The **Wieliczka Salt Mine** is hundreds of years old and was once a primary "money pillar" for the Polish Kingdom. I booked the **Miner’s Route**, which was an amazing hands-on experience! I actually got to mine salt and learn about the grueling daily lives of the historical miners.

![selfie](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1771184006/IMG_1913_oboojv.jpg)


**Fun Fact:** Did you know the word *"Salary"* comes from *"Salt"*? 😲 In Roman times, soldiers were often paid in salt. Here is my "salary" for the day! 👇

## Wawel Castle & The Dragon 🐉🏰

By the time I reached the Castle, it was too late to go inside, but the exterior walk was stunning. **Wawel Cathedral** is a beautiful "chaos" of design—it wasn't built all at once, so you can see different architectural styles clashing across the centuries. 🏗️

I noticed most rooftops here are a distinct green. Originally, these were iron/copper and turned green through oxidation. Eventually, this became a status symbol! People began using pre-oxidized materials just to show off how "old" and expensive their buildings were. ✨

![Wawel Cathedral](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1771185655/IMG_1975_yojo7o.jpg)

Finally, I visited the **Wawel Dragon** sculpture. Legend says a dragon used to eat the locals until a clever man fed it a lamb skin stuffed with sulfur. The dragon ate it, got so thirsty he drank half the Vistula River, and exploded! 💥

![Dragon](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1771185657/IMG_1995_w2jlei.jpg)

The sculpture usually spits real fire every few minutes, but I think it was too cold today—the dragon stayed quiet. 🥶🔥

Fun tip: 胡芦娃(Kurwa) is a curse word in Polish.
