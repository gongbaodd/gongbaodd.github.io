# Milan: The Most Chinese-Friendly City in Italy? 🇮🇹🇨🇳

I only had a whirlwind **24 hours** in Milan. Arriving at 10:00 PM and leaving at 8:00 AM two days later meant I had to make every second count! 🏃‍♂️ My hostel was near the Central Station, which was convenient, but my bus out was from Lampugnano—a 30-minute metro ride away. 🚇

### A Walk Down Memory Lane 🛍️

Walking from my hostel toward the **Duomo di Milano**, I passed through **Porta Venezia**. The street felt like one giant open-air shopping center. Seeing vendors selling discounted clothes right on the sidewalk felt like a flashback to China in the early 2000s! It’s nostalgic because back home, that culture has mostly vanished in favor of online shopping. 📱📦

### Is this Milan or Shanghai? 🏙️

Milan is Italy’s economic powerhouse, and the skyscrapers definitely give off that vibe. With the **Winter Olympics** in full swing, the "Chinese connection" was everywhere. ❄️

* **Sponsorships:** Posters for **TCL** and **Alibaba** are plastered all over the city.
* **The Logo:** This year’s Olympic logo looks remarkably like the Chinese character **“功”** (Gōng).
* **The Vibe:** I even saw locals filming **TikToks** in traditional Chinese clothing! For a moment, I honestly felt like I was back in Shanghai. 🇨🇳✨

---

### The Iconic Landmarks ⛪

**Duomo di Milano**
The massive white cathedral is perhaps the only thing that reminds you you're definitely *not* in Shanghai. The level of detail is insane! 😲 Looking at the thousands of sculptures, I couldn't help but wonder: if a huge gust of wind hits, do any of them blow down? From a distance, the church actually looks like a giant, elaborate display stand for action figures. 🧸

**Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II**
Right next door is this famous shopping gallery. If it weren't in the heart of Milan, it would look exactly like the **Yiwu Leather City** back home! 🐂 I saw the famous bull mosaic on the floor—legend says if you spin three times on your right heel on the bull's testicles, you'll have good luck. There was a massive queue of people waiting for their turn! 🍀

---

### History & Culture 🏰

* **Teatro alla Scala:** A quick stop to see the famous opera house and the **Leonardo da Vinci** statue standing proudly in front. 🎭
* **Castello Sforzesco:** A real castle with perfectly preserved walls! It’s a bit small, but very cool. Behind it lies a beautiful garden leading to the **Arco della Pace**, where the Olympic torch is currently held. 🔥
* **Chinatown:** Unlike many "Chinatowns" that feel like 1920s Hong Kong, Milan’s Chinatown feels like **modern China**. It’s authentic and bustling. 🥢
* **Cimitero Monumentale:** Located just north of Chinatown, this isn't just a cemetery; it’s an open-air sculpture museum. The artistry of the tombs is breathtaking. 🗿

---

### Living Like a Local 🛒

Before heading back, I stopped at **Esselunga**—the supermarket locals swear by. The variety was incredible! It really made me realize how good the quality of life is in Western Europe. 🧀🍷

I finished my night at the **Milano Centrale** station. The building was beautifully lit up in the colors of the Italian flag (green, white, and red) 🇮🇹, featuring a giant apple sculpture and a **TCL Olympic showroom** right in front. After a quick grocery run to Lidl and Pam—where the shelves were almost empty!—I headed back to prep for another busy day of travel. 😴
