# 🇪🇸 Barcelona: Sea and Gaudi's canvas

### 😴 The Rude Awakening

I finally rolled into Barcelona at 7:00 AM. One minute I was deep in a dream, the next, the driver was shouting **“Sants!”** 📢 Before I knew it, I was standing on the curb with my luggage, watching the bus disappear into the distance. 🏃‍♂️💨

I found myself surrounded by tents that looked like a local street settlement. With police cars patrolling the area, I couldn’t help but feel like I blended right in with my messy "just woke up" look! 👮‍♂️😅 Luckily, my hostel host was a legend and saved my bed so I could check in early. 🙌

### 🌊 The Magic of the Coast

![selfie](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1770506104/IMG_0996_cebupd.jpg)

Even with the rough start, the moment I stepped out, I remembered why **seaside cities are always the best.** 🏝️ There’s a special magic in the air when you're near the water. Even though a train cancellation left me with only one day to explore, that salty breeze made me feel like I could conquer the whole city in 24 hours! 🌬️💙

---

### ⛲️ Parc de la Ciutadella

![Parc de la Ciutadella](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1770506107/IMG_1025_r48myd.jpg)

I started at the **Parc de la Ciutadella**. The fountain there is massive, though it honestly gave me major nostalgia—it looks exactly like those luxury spas back in China in the early 2000s! ⛲️✨

---

### ⛪️ The Main Event: Sagrada Família

![Sagrada Família](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1770506110/IMG_1058_hhfeq6.jpg)

The walk to the **Sagrada Família** was a struggle—the clouds opened up and it started pouring rain! 🌧️ I was soaked, but as soon as I reached the foot of the church and lifted my phone to take a selfie, the sky literally split open. ☀️✨

The sun came out right at that moment! It felt like a total "divine" coincidence. Looking up at Gaudí’s masterpiece, the details are mind-blowing. ❤️‍🔥

---

### 🏙️ Streets & Sweets

![Passeig de Gràcia](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1770506107/IMG_1071_isauew.jpg)

Walking down **Passeig de Gràcia**, I realized Barcelona’s architecture is on another level. 🏛️ Truthfully? I enjoyed it way more than the Champs-Élysées in Paris! Every building has its own personality. 🎨

![La Boqueria](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1770506111/IMG_1092_clf3kj.jpg)

To wrap up the day, I hit the **La Boqueria** market right by my hostel. 🦐🍎 It’s a sensory overload in the best way possible—the perfect spot to grab some fresh fish and local snacks to celebrate surviving my chaotic morning! 🥂🐟
