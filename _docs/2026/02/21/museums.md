### ⭐ Top Pick: Tallinn Museum of Orders of Knighthood

![Tallinn Museum of Orders of Knighthood](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1771865025/IMG_2076_ambrnz.jpg)

I **strongly suggest** visiting this one! It’s a specialized, intimate museum packed with stunning medals from all over the globe—even from Asia 🌏. I was surprised to find **Manchukuo medals** here!

**A few tips for your visit:**

* **History Fans:** This is a total "must-visit" 🏛️.
* **Tech Buddy:** Using **AI** while browsing helped me dive deep into the historical context of the pieces. 🤖📖
* **Timing:** The museum is small. If you just want to look at the medals, you’ll be done in a few minutes. But if you're a history buff, give yourself time to soak it all in! ✨
