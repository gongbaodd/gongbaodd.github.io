# 🇨🇿 Finally Found the Wishing Well in Prague! ⛲️

I hopped on a **RegioJet** bus from Munich to Prague, and honestly, I was impressed! 🚌 The bus felt brand new, complete with chargers and working Wi-Fi. 🔋💻

![window seat](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1771106133/IMG_1573_xt6zub.jpg)

## 📶 The "Lost Signal" Struggle

Just before crossing into Czechia, my signal vanished! 📵 I had to scramble to check if my data plan supported roaming. Pro-tip: I actually had to switch my network settings to **T-Mobile / O2** to get back online. 📱🌐

## 🚋 Vintage Vibes & Pop Culture

Prague still runs those charming old tram models. 🚃 They felt so familiar! These **PCC-style** trams used to be popular all over the world, and seeing them here is like a blast from the past. 🏛️

![tram](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1771106143/IMG_1687_bvf2eb.jpg)

Most Chinese travelers know Prague from Jolin Tsai’s famous song *Prague Square* (布拉格广场). 🎤 I’ll admit, I was a little disappointed at first—the huge square in front of the museum is currently under metro construction. 🚧🏗️

![metro construction](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1771106133/IMG_1588_hjakzp.jpg)

## 🍺 Groceries & "Herb Cola"

**Albert** is the go-to local supermarket here. 🛒 Fun fact: beer is actually cheaper than water! 🍺💧 I also made sure to pick up some **JoJo** gummy candies. 🍬

| beer | candy |
|-|-|
|![beer](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1771106136/IMG_1592_vwi8bt.jpg) | ![candy](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1771106136/IMG_1593_cadxbe.jpg) |

I also stopped by **Lidl**, which used to be an old bank vault! 🏦 There’s a massive lock kept as a memorial. I tried **Kofola**, the local "herb cola." It tastes a bit like Dr. Pepper... 🥤🌿

![vault](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1771106133/IMG_1633_kmmjy8.jpg)

> **Budget Tip:** The exchange rate is roughly **25 CZK : 1 EUR** (or 3:1 for RMB). Compared to the rest of Europe, Czechia feels much more affordable! 💰📉

## 🎭 The Legend vs. Reality

![Old Town Square](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1771106136/IMG_1645_r1ntqz.jpg)

I walked from Wenceslas Square (Václvské náměstí) to the **Old Town Square**. Everyone gathers at the **Prague Astronomical Clock** (Pražský orloj) for the puppet show when the clock strikes the hour. 🕒🐥

## 🏰 Crossing the Vltava

![Charles Bridge](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1771106136/IMG_1613_teczb4.jpg)

I crossed the **Charles Bridge** (Karlův most), making sure to touch the sculptures for good luck. 🖐️✨ I moved from the Old Town into **Malá Strana** (Lesser Town). Honestly, the "Lesser Town Square" looks more like a parking lot than a square. 🅿️ And yep... still no wishing well. 🤷‍♂️

![Lesser Town Square](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1771106134/IMG_1628_tv2bet.jpg)

* **Franz Kafka Sculpture:** This giant mirrored head rotates every hour. It’s mesmerizing! 🤖👤

![The Hanging Men](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1771106136/IMG_1662_f0nb0u.jpg)

* **The Hanging Men:** On the way to the **Dancing House**, I spotted two sculptures of men hanging over the street. 🕴️ It’s a powerful metaphor for an unstable life. 🏗️
* **Kynute:** I found these! They are basically Czech *mantou*. 🥟 Way tastier than regular bread! 😋

## ⛲️ The Discovery!

After crossing **Most Legií**, I visited the **Lennon Wall** and tried to see the famous "narrowest street" (sadly, it was closed). 🎨🚧

![wishing well](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1771106140/IMG_1710_kbe8xa.jpg)

But then... I found it! **The Wishing Well at the Kafka Museum!** ⛲️✨ No wonder the lyrics say, *"The view was too beautiful, I didn't dare to look."* (那画面太美我不敢看). Success at last! 🎯

## 🏰 Castle Views

![changing of guard](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1771106140/IMG_1731_jkakqe.jpg)

I started the climb up to **Prague Castle**. 🏰 Most of the main sights are free to walk through! Since the gardens are closed in February, there wasn't *too* much walking, but I caught the **Changing of the Guard**. 💂‍♂️⚔️

I visited the **Golden Lane**—I'm not sure why they charge for it during the day, but it’s **free after 16:00**, so I waited and explored it then. 🏠🔑

## 🛤️ Final Thoughts

Prague reminds me a lot of **Qingdao**. 🌊 The train station is a modest building because most of the infrastructure is hidden underground. 🚉 On my train out, they even offered free comic books to read! 📖🎈
