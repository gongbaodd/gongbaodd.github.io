# Week 39: Muon & Quantum

This week's pyData Tallinn is a must visit. Because it is about hard core physics.

![Agenda](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1790850978/IMG_3924_legthf.jpg)

The first one, GScan is to use muon to scan the break parts of the concrete. I listened to their presentation on [the CV meetup](/life/2026/07/02/cv-tallinn/) in July. That was awesome, this time Mihkail introduced how they made a simulation to test scaning and how to manage communications between scientists and programmers.

The second one, is about quantum computing. Enefit got a chance to use IBM's quantum computing in one hackathon. He uses [Hamiltonian System 哈密頓系統](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_system) to identify the lowest-price charging solution for electric cars. From my understanding, it is more like a chip in the circuit for quantum computing.
