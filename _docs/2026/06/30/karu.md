# Week 26: Moving out of Karu

Last day in Karu. Last month, I was fully working on my PhD application. Which made me no time to prepare a new place to live 😓.

However, the dorm manager provides me a place in Räägu. It is quite place and very near to [the rock of Tondi](/life/2026/06/07/raku). It is way cheaper to live here. There is a Lidl and a Rimi super markets around. They both larger than the ones in Karu. Although Räägu is no wehre near the sea, can not read paper on the beach😭. Also, only communal kitchen and toilet are provided. However, pretty good for transfering.

Anyway, Karu is in the city center, you can go to any event by walking. While Räägu is more like in the neighbourhood. Low expense cost, going to events needs some time.

However, the last few days in this week, I will more focuse on some thing I like. (It is not like I do not like my PhD topic 🙃). Just something that do not need to read too many readings but still important in the industry. There are still a lot untaught in my last two years' study.
