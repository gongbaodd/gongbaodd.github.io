# Hiking in the Raku Lake

It has been two years since I passed by the rock of Tondi. Somehow, it was my very first sightseeing about Tallinn when I searched online.

![Tondi](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1780855035/65FB38E1-72F0-4A59-B2E4-2D7B9EF9C22E_csjgai.jpg)

After tram 3 I turned to bus 57 got to Raku station. The lake is about 1 km away.

![Sand beach](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1780854296/1cda88aa-c3fa-495e-a9d2-037ab8515a97_tieyly.jpg)
