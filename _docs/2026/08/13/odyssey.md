From my point view is like, Odysseus compainioned with his comrades to invade a country, which is against so called "Zeus Law". And then on the way back, he hope others can treat him with the "Zeus Law". Of course, they did not. At the same time, his own country got backfired as there is too long time for the king, Odysseus left.

Many people compared this with The journey to the west(西游记). Well, after watching the movie. I think they are talking two different things. The Odyssey is certainly about a topic in Chinese called "礼坏乐崩". Old rules are desprecated, new rules are waiting to be formed.

Before watching this movie, I thought it would be a story of going home.
As I am also on the way home of my abroad study. I would like to get some spark from it.
