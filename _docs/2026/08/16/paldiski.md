# Paldiski: Cliff, Lighthouse and Blue Berries

![Paldiski](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1786907693/IMG_3401_qrxj8f.jpg)

Today, we hiked Paldiski, a harbour under Tallinn. This time I can see some formal cliffs. 

I have to take the elron train to go outside Tallinn. Turns out the trip outside Tallinn can not paid by the Tallinn bus card. Still a bank card is needed. I just have discount.

![beach](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1786907692/IMG_3360_pd2bo4.jpg)

![beach](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1786907692/IMG_3363_algjw3.jpg)

Anout 90 minutes hiking along the cliff. We saw the lighthouse. 5 EUR to go up.

![Lighthouse](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1786907693/IMG_3385_zlwuys.jpg)

This harbour has deep water, so here are a lot power plants, not only wind generator, also solar farm and a huge storage looks like gas or petrol.

![Powerplant](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1786907692/IMG_3392_bmy5vp.jpg)

![grassland](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1786907692/IMG_3391_mibkjo.jpg)

On the way to the train station, there are blue berries everywhere. I was eating the whole way.

![blueberry](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1786907692/IMG_3395_1_dqihpp.jpg)

It was raining on the train back. This is the huge rainbow ar Hiiu station.

![Hiiu](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1786907693/IMG_3408_bjuqmn.jpg)
