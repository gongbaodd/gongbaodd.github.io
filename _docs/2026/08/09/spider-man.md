# Spider Man: Brand New Day

![poster](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1786299296/images_cpwk82.jpg)

First film after I decide to give up PhD. Great choice. Somehow Marvel movies are good again.

The story is focusing on how people handle change. Peter Parker was forgotten by everyone. Jean Grey lost her sister.
They have to face the new state. Finally, the villan and the character have the same purpose.
By comparing them, people can see why a hero is a hero.

I really like how the movie handles the talk part. Yes, the villan was talked to yield. Well, it is Jean Grey, she can not be died.
There will be more movies about her. This time the talk part is done by aunt May, from Peter's memory. Genius idea!
