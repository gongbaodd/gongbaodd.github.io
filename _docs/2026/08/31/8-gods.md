# All Wishes come true! 八仙！

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1788188309/2b53c7c3-0e9e-452e-a4a4-b41a0cfd43eb_kzuacp.jpg)

This is quite anticipated movie that I want to see. Some how the eight gods are related to my website. As the website was firstly made for my graduation trip. And my first station was Penglai.

The original story is totally rewritten into a new story. The movie just uses the theme. I am not against that. As the story of eight gods are too separated. One movie probably is not enough. So I like the story.

However, many twists are not reasonable. It is true there is a huge twist in the end of the story that explained everything. But in retrospect, the time when the characters made decisions, they do not know the final twist. As do I. So I can only say they have very deep friendships?

Anyway, very good scenes, amazing fighting scenes and a workable story as long as you do not dig too deep. The movie is still like 4 stars enjoyable.
