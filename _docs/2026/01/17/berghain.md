# Berghain

Berlin consistently caters to my aesthetic and subculture interests. Staying near **Urban Nation** is ideal; the surrounding blocks are filled with impressive street art and hidden fetish boutiques that define the city's edgy character.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1768950769/IMG_9296_ktcfpf.jpg)

For a quick bite, **Curry 36** is a local staple. You can't go wrong with their classic Currywurst—steamed then fried sausages smothered in spiced tomato sauce and curry powder.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1768950767/IMG_9304_n51hun.jpg)

Then, there is **Berghain**. More than just a world-class nightclub, it houses **Lab.oratory**, a dedicated cruising club downstairs. It is a must-visit for those seeking a raw, "modern factory punk" atmosphere that feels like a gritty, Roman-esque scene brought into the 21st century.
