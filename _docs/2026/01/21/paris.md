# Paris, uhh... Strike! 🇫🇷🥖

I stayed at a place called **The People** located right by the **Nation** station. Paris is massive, and wouldn't you know it—I arrived just as they updated the ticket system! 🎫


### 🚇 Quick Survival Guide: Transport & Costs

* **Metro:** €2.55 per trip (valid for 2 hours).
* **Bus/Tram:** €2.05 (valid for 1.5 hours). You can transfer, but you can’t take the same line back! 🚌
* **Pro Tip:** Ticket prices for major attractions have also gone up. If you're staying central, **walking** is your best friend. For long trips like the **Palace of Versailles**, the train is definitely the budget-friendly move. 👟
* **Shoutout:** A kind local gentleman helped me navigate the app while I was busy crunching numbers to find the cheapest route! 📱👴

### 🚩 The "Monday Strike" Pivot

I was happily heading to the **Louvre** when I heard the news: **Strike!** 🪧 Since the Louvre is closed on Tuesdays, striking on a Monday effectively gives people a 4-day weekend. I get it, Mondays are hard, but it meant a sudden change of plans! 😅

I turned my day into a "Speedrun of Central Paris" to see as many spots as possible before my 90-minute ticket expired. 🏃💨

**Notre-Dame de Paris** sits on an island accessible by beautiful bridges. The old prison (Conciergerie) is also right there. I then swung by the

#### 🌳 Gardens and Squares

In front of the museum lies the **Tuileries Garden**—a lovely spot with free green chairs where you can relax by the pond. ⛲️

![Arc de Triomphe du Carrousel](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1769190584/IMG_9488_ih4h8d.jpg)

At the end of the garden is **Place de la Concorde**. It’s famous for its Egyptian obelisk, but history buffs know it as the site of the guillotine. From here, you can see the **Eiffel Tower** peeking out in the distance! 🗼

![obelisk](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1769190584/IMG_9497_lch1wa.jpg)

### 👑 The Grandeur of Versailles

The next day, I headed to **Versailles**. Despite having a 9:30 AM ticket, I faced a "comedy of errors": getting stuck in ticket machines, taking the wrong direction, and ending up on the wrong train! 🚂 (Pro tip: The L-line has *many* branches).

The train cabin was beautiful, though, and the window view was worth the struggle. Even though I was an hour late, they still let me in! 🙌

![metro cabin](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1769190595/IMG_9576_tatpnd.jpg)

![window view](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1769190596/IMG_9585_jymcqa.jpg)

In winter, many rooms are closed, so I spent most of my time finding "meme-worthy" paintings. 🖼️

![Meme](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1769190597/IMG_9602_j73vcf.jpg)

**Hot Take:** People often compare Versailles to the **Forbidden City**. Honestly? The Forbidden City is a whole city of multiple palaces; I still think it's the more impressive visit. 🇨🇳🏯


#### 🤳 AR & The Gardens
