# Köln: My First Glimpse of Western Europe

### 🚄 The Journey from Berlin

I hopped on the **ICE train** from Berlin to Cologne. Pro tip for fellow travelers: like most Deutsche Bahn (DB) journeys, many people skip the extra seat reservation fee. Just look for the small **LED screens** above the seats—if it’s blank, it’s all yours! 🎫💨

### ⛪️ A Grand Welcome: The Kölner Dom

I definitely should have stayed longer! The moment I stepped out of the station, the **Cologne Cathedral** hit me. Even though I knew it was right there, the sheer scale of this 13th-century masterpiece (finally finished in the 19th century!) is breathtaking. That massive, dark Gothic silhouette is enough to leave anyone in total awe. 😲⛪️🖤

![Cologne Cathedral](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1769180589/IMG_9351_utn828.jpg)

### 🌍 A City of Expression

Cologne immediately showed its vibrant side. I walked right into a massive **Iranian parade**. At first glance, the colors reminded me of the Italian flag, but the Arabic script quickly clarified things. It’s clear why this city is known as the **media hub of Germany**—it’s full of voice and energy! 📢🇮🇷📺

![The parade](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1769180019/IMG_9337_lsf8bn.jpg)

The city’s openness is everywhere. I was struck by the bold, inclusive atmosphere—huge posters celebrating the **LGBTQ+ community** are a common and welcoming sight. 🌈🏳️‍🌈✨

![LGBT poster](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1769180588/IMG_9356_p4b8r8.jpg)

### 🏘️ Small Town Vibes, Big History

Despite its importance, Cologne feels like a **delicate old town**. There’s a "neighborly" vibe, where it feels like everyone knows everyone. Hearing the Cathedral bells chime every hour reminded me of the ancient atmosphere in some towns in **Shanxi province**. It’s a beautiful mix of history and cozy living. 🔔🏡🏮


![hostel](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1769181086/IMG_9360_sjgqcz.jpg)

### ❄️ The Chill Factor

One thing to note: Cologne is surprisingly chilly! 🥶 Even though the thermometer said **10°C** (much warmer than the 0°C I left in Berlin), the damp air made me shiver. Pack layers! 🧥🧣

![The model in the train station](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1769180968/IMG_9381_zww96g.jpg)

### 💭 Final Thoughts

Cologne is such a lovely, compact city. I only planned for one afternoon this time, but I’ve already decided—I’m coming back for a much deeper exploration next time! 🎒👋💖
