# Bruxelles: From Comic Books to the EU


### The Classic "DB Experience" 🚆

Typical **Deutsche Bahn**—my train to Brussels was delayed. Traveling from Cologne, I first arrived at **Brussels North**. I learned that my transfer ticket to **Brussels Central** was valid for 24 hours, meaning I could hop on any train heading that way.

The challenge? The platform screens were strictly in French and Dutch, and neither mentioned "Brussels Central" as a stop (it's usually a passing station). Thanks to a quick assist from **Gemini**, I managed to navigate the linguistic puzzle and find the right platform! 💡

![The platform](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1769183729/IMG_9391_gy5f2p.jpg)


### Hills, Statues, and Grand Squares 🏛️

I made a slight tactical error today. Cologne was so walkable that I assumed Brussels would be the same. I was wrong—this city has a lot of hills to climb! 🏃‍♂️💨

I started at the **Grand Place**, which is just a short walk from the Central Station. The square is absolutely breathtaking! Nearby, I tracked down the famous **Manneken Pis** (pissing boy) and **Jeanneke Pis** (pissing girl) sculptures.

![Grand Place](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1769184353/IMG_9403_trlepg.jpg)


### A Change of Plans: The Comic Museum 🎨

The **Comic Art Museum** wasn’t originally in my schedule. I only had a 3-4 hour window in the city, and it seemed a bit out of the way. However, I realized I’d regret skipping it, so I made a last-minute detour. It was definitely worth the stop! 📚

![Comic museum](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1769184689/IMG_9417_berypr.jpg)

### Scientific History at the EU Council 🇪🇺

Next, I headed over to the **EU Council**. Since it was Sunday, the area was peaceful and empty. But the real goal wasn't just the politics—it was science history.

![EU council](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1769185309/IMG_9430_sx0apg.jpg)

I was searching for the location of "[The Most Intelligent Picture Ever Taken](https://rarehistoricalphotos.com/solvay-conference-probably-intelligent-picture-ever-taken-1927/)" (the 1927 Solvay Conference). It was taken right near the EU Council buildings. Standing where Einstein and Curie once stood felt legendary. 🧪🧠

![the Most Intelligent Picture Ever Taken](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1769185577/IMG_9434_rwwgm8.jpg)

### Quick Bites & Station Vibes 🍟

Everyone in Brussels seems to be eating **Belgian fries** (Fritkot). While it's the local staple, I skipped them this time—mostly because I was still working through a giant amout sausages I brought from Berlin! 🌭

### Next Stop: Paris with OuiGo 🇫🇷

My journey continued from **Brussels South** (Midi/Zuid). This station is huge, handling everything from local lines to the Eurostar.

![Brussel Midi](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1769186423/IMG_9439_cqjs94.jpg)


I took the **OuiGo** to Paris, which is a budget traveler's dream if you're prepared. They use "second-hand" trains and charge extra for everything—luggage, power outlets, and Wi-Fi. However, the seats are surprisingly spacious! The desk was actually large enough to hold my laptop comfortably, which is rare for European trains. 💻

**Tip for travelers:** I arrived an hour early expecting a security pre-check, but there wasn't one. Also, keep a sharp eye on the platform marquees—the information scrolls by quickly and is very easy to miss! 🚉
