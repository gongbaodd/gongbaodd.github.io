# Week 3: San Sebastián 🌊

After a 5-hour train ride, I finally arrived in **Hendaye**. Fun fact: the name sounds a lot like "Hentai," but luckily the "H" is silent! 🙊 This is the gateway to Spain, where I hopped on the **Euskotren**—a charming narrow-gauge railway—to reach San Sebastián. 🚉

![Window view](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1769200027/IMG_9740_awyjte.jpg)

By the time I pulled into San Sebastián, it was late and raining. 🌧️ The humid air and the city's initial vibe actually reminded me a lot of Thailand! 🇹🇭

![Bridge](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1769200031/IMG_9751_mhp7jx.jpg)

My first meal? A simple salad paired with my first-ever **Dr. Pepper**. Honestly? Not a fan. 🥤🚫

![Dr. Pepper](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1769200027/IMG_9754_odznzw.jpg)

### City Vibes & AI Guides 🤖

The next morning, the weather cleared up beautifully. This is exactly the European city aesthetic I was hoping for! ✨

![Rainbow](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1769200029/IMG_9766_obrzyb.jpg)

The local beaches are a canvas; you can find artists creating intricate sand drawings that change day by day. 🏖️🎨

**The verdict?** Doubao is more chatty with information, while Gemini feels more intuitive to interact with. However, you always have to fact-check! Doubao tried to convince me a regular tree was a sculpture. I’m pretty sure it was just... a tree. 🌳😂

And the network signal was not very stable. Sometime you lost connection while shopping. 🛍️

### Surfing & Game of Thrones ⚔️🏄‍♂️

San Sebastián also served as a filming location for HBO's **Game of Thrones**. Walking along the coast, the rugged landscape feels instantly familiar. 🐉

![land](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1769200030/IMG_9834_zsgpse.jpg)

As a huge surfing fan, I was in heaven. Even at 15°C, the water was filled with surfers catching waves. 🏄‍♂️❄️ The Old Town is packed with cool surf shops and local character.
