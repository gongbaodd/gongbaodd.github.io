# Week 0: My Yearly Workout Wrap-up! 🎆💪

Happy New Year, everyone! ✨ This was actually my first time catching the fireworks in **Tallinn**! 🇪🇪 Last year I was stuck in the suburbs pet-sitting 🐶, so this was a nice change of pace.

I’ll be honest—I didn’t do much "tech digging" this week. Most of my energy went into practicing German 🇩🇪 and grinding through work. But, I realized I still have one big topic to chat about: **My fitness journey.** 🏋️‍♂️


---

### Switching things up from CrossFit 🔄

For the last five years, I’ve been a loyal follower of the [CrossFit WOD](https://www.crossfit.com/workout/). But lately? Not so much. Keeping up with a complex WOD in a crowded gym is a nightmare when people are hogging all the gear 🙄. Plus, the workouts started feeling a bit too easy.

I decided it was time for a custom plan. My goal? **Get a massive chest and wider shoulders.** 🦍

* **Chest:** Swapped the barbell for dumbbell bench presses (way better mind-muscle connection for me! 📈).
* **Shoulders:** Spamming lateral raises for that width.
* **Core:** Yoga ball sit-ups and kettlebell rows to keep the midsection tight. ✨

### The "Thick Thigh" Problem 👖

I used to be obsessed with heavy squats and deadlifts, but I ran into a weird problem: **My legs got too thick.** 🍗 Seriously, I can barely find trousers that fit anymore! To lean them out, I’ve been doing trunk-carry running. Sadly, that means I’m putting thrusters, cleans, and jerks on the back burner for now. 🥲

 

### Getting back to my "Roots" 🍆😂

I recently hopped on the hip abductor machine and it hit me... I remembered why I started gymming in the first place. It wasn't just to look good—**it was to get my morning wood back!** 🪵

Around age 27–28, I noticed things weren't as "upbeat" as they used to be. I discovered that heavy leg movements are like a natural cheat code for blood flow and libido. 🚀 So, to get back to those original goals, I’m swapping the running for **trunk-carry step-ups**. Keeping the gains, but focusing on the *functionality* I care about. 😉
