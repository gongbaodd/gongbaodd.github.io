# The HuggingFace Agents Course

This is a fundamental [agent course from huggingface](https://huggingface.co/learn/agents-course/en/unit0/introduction).
It can be regard as a supplement of [the AI course](/series/aisoft-course/) I learnt this June. As the agent part of that course was too shallow.

While I am traveling in Finland. I made the course into a [pdf](https://drive.google.com/file/d/15ycRi6D-ez6P0YEP9_Mzws0iBQp1eXJK/view?usp=sharing) to read.

For me the introduction part is too easy. The framework part is quite interesting. It compared smolagents, LlamaIndex and LangGraph. And how to evaluate them.

The bonus units are also a good to read.
