# Aalto University: Designs for a Cooler Planet

This is my last night in Finland. I decide to go to Aalto University to see the English tour of their exhibition `Design for a Cooler Planet`.

![selfie](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1793130407/IMG_4447_gpsk0z.jpg)

This is the project that uses waste material to make inks or foams.
I found it interesting as the modern food chains/fashion chains are more profitable when making wastes. 
This reuse method can be a valuable usage to ballance reusing waste and keep the products' price.
