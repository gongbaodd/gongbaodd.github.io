# Last stop, Suomenlinna

Suomenlinna is a sea fortress in Finland. It was built by sweden and then Russia.

The [first time](/life/2025/07/27/helsinki/) I went to Finland. I met a group of people, they went to hiking in Suomenlinna. But I do not have time to go with them. So this place became my must visit for my second visit to Finland. 

To go to the island, ferries are needed. It is included in HSL A-B ticket. It takes 30min for the trip.

If you are a history fan or a weapon fun, you will like this place. 

For me, this place feels more like the Great Wall. Like the place `King's Gate`, you can go up down through the fortress. It was so much fun.
