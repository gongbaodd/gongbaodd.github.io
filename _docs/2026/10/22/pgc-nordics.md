# Wrap up my PGC Nordics 2026

![Grandpa's bee haven on PGC](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1792660840/helsinki-grandpa_ba1njg.jpg)

Come and play [Grandpa's Bee Haven](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3209160/Grandpas_Bee_Haven/), only 6.99 EUR.

Last two days, I was in Helsinki attending [Pocket Gamers Connect](https://www.pgconnects.com/nordics/schedule/). As [Grandpa's Bee Haven](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3209160/Grandpas_Bee_Haven/) is selected in [The Big Indie Pitch](https://www.pgconnects.com/nordics/the-big-indie-pitch-mobile-edition/).


![PGC Nordics](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1792660697/IMG_4254_omyf0y.jpg)

## New trends

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1792664394/IMG_4178_ighvmx.jpg)
