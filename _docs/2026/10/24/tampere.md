# The Finnish Game Museum

Set foot to Tampere! After I went to [Estonian Game Library](/plan/2024/08/31/week-35-orientation-week/), I realized that there is a game library in Finland. However, most of the museums in Finland are settled in Tampere.

It is quite easy to book a ticket on VR. The ticket price floats from 15~20 from Helsinki to Tampere. Normally the bus tickets are cheaper, but the bus tickets usually charge service fee. I got the last ticket to Tampere, at least the website shows so. However, the train was not as crowded as I expected.

![Helsinki Railway Station](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1792843434/IMG_4289_w3dnrz.jpg)

The first impression of Tampere. Not a big city, I can see the edge of the city from the train station.

![Tampere](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1792843435/IMG_4296_tvuv5n.jpg)
