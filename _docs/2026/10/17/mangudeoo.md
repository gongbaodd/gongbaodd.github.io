# Week 41: MängudeÖÖ 2026

While writing this post, I am in heavy fever. After [digiConf](/tech/2026/10/09/digi/) I caught cold. I thought I got better, so I went to MängudeÖÖ.

[MängudeÖÖ](https://mangudeoo.ee/en/konverents/) is an Estonia Expo that holds yearly. This year they include a lot VR games.

[Puzzeling Places](https://www.meta.com/experiences/puzzling-places/3931148300302917/) and [Ancient Dungeon](https://www.meta.com/experiences/ancient-dungeon/5138511912885491/) make talks about their marketing experience. Turns out, there are 20% of people are 65+ playing puzzeling places.

I also played [UnLoop](https://www.meta.com/experiences/unloop/7453152428032973) from CM games. In this game, you copy yourself to solve puzzels.

I played [White Phosphorus: An Explosive Escape](https://kaeri-gg.itch.io/white-phosphorus-an-explosive-escape), a game made by TLU's students, one of the maker is also from China.

[Chicks on the menu](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5253290/Chicks_on_the_Menu/), a 1v1 hide and sick game.

[Machine Party](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4108000/Machine_Party/), A multiplayer battle royale game.

I could not finish all the events. As the fever got worse. I still have one workshop from [iGame project](https://cocreation.uni.igameproject.eu) wanted to attend. Maybe next time.

Also, come and play the our game, [Grandpa's Bee Haven](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3209160/Grandpas_Bee_Haven/).
