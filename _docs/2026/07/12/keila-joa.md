# Keila-Joa Waterfall and Beach

Last month was too busy for me. Also at the same time, all my friends are vacationing. 
One of my friend asked me to go to Keila-Joa Waterfall with her.

![waterfall](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1784011708/IMG_2989_opdygk.jpg)

It is about 60 min bus to there. It is a 6 meter waterfall with a castle next to it.
The castle and the garden area is 19 EUR a visit. So we took a photo of the front door. Where is the sculpture's butt.

![front door](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1784011708/IMG_3002_i1pehk.jpg)

The beach is not far. To go to the beach, needs to walk across the forest. Remember to wear trousers. There are many people swimming in the water. 
Like all the beaches around Tallinn, the water is very shallow.

![bunker](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1784011708/IMG_3015_i5apbx.jpg)

There are not so many busses to Tallinn at 19:00. But you can plan to go to Keila and take the train. We are lucky that we caught the last bus to Tallinn.
