# Week 28: I Do NOT like moving 😭

Last month, [I moved out of Karu](/plan/2026/06/30/karu). As I was busy on my PhD Application, I asked that if I can be transferred to Räägu dorm.

Still, my contract can only hold for a month. So last week, I was busy looking for a new place for me. Eventually, I found this hostel Larsen.ee, which is also suggested by my colleague.

The room is not big, about 16 $m^2$. And half of it was the bathroom. There is a murphy bed, so I can fold it when I do not sleep.

Each room, there is a poster in front of it. Mine is parasite. Which is kind of funny. The only asian poster, and I am probably the only eastern asian in the floor.

![Parasite](https://res.cloudinary.com/dmq8ipket/image/upload/v1784549471/IMG_3038_xtjwlq.jpg)

There is a co-working place. I believe I will use that a lot, considering the small room. And a rooftop, where can see the sea view.

Lidl is just right next to the building I live. This is actually a very good news for me.

The total expense, rent + facilities + electricity ... are around 500 EUR. It is more expansive, I remember my classmates two years ago, he rent a room like this. It only costed him 440 EUR, and it was in the city center.

After moving for one day, I am exhausted. Next two weeks, I will be teaching at school. I need to change one bus to get to school. Like 40 minutes' traffic.
